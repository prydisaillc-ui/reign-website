Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Plain-English summary: Businesses own their client data; we use it only to run the platform for them. We don't sell personal information. We never share mobile numbers or text-message consent with third parties for their marketing. You can ask for your data or its deletion at any time.

1. Who we are

Reign OS ("Reign," "we," "us") is a booking and business platform for service businesses, operated by ReignOS LLC, Decatur, Georgia, United States. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect information when you use the Reign website, booking pages and platform (the "Service").

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: name, business name, email address and password.
  • Business profile: services, prices, hours, location and social links.
  • Client details submitted on a booking page or added by a business: name, email, mobile number, notes and booking history.
  • Payment details, collected by Stripe on our behalf. Reign never stores full card numbers.
  • Messages you send to our support team.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data, such as pages visited and features used.
  • Device and browser data, such as IP address, browser type and operating system.
  • Cookies needed to keep you signed in and secure (see section 9).

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, secure and improve the Service.
  • Run booking pages and send booking messages by email and, where a client has opted in, by text message.
  • Process subscription billing and booking deposits.
  • Provide AI assistant features using the business's own information.
  • Send product and account emails to business owners; promotional emails can be turned off at any time.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce our Terms and meet legal obligations.

4. Text messages (SMS)

If a client checks the text-message consent box when booking, Reign sends transactional texts about their appointments: confirmations, reminders, reschedules and cancellations. Consent is optional and is not required to book. Message frequency varies (usually 2–4 per booking). Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. See our SMS Terms.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We use Twilio only as our service provider to deliver these messages on our behalf.

5. Client data belongs to the business

Client records created through bookings or imported by a business ("Client Data") belong to that business. Reign processes Client Data only on the business's instructions, as a data processor. We do not sell, rent or share Client Data with any third party for its own marketing. Each business is responsible for having a lawful basis to use its clients' information and for honoring its clients' requests.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that run parts of the Service for us, under contracts that limit their use of the data: Stripe (payments), Twilio (text messages), Resend (email), AI model providers such as OpenAI (assistant features) and cloud hosting providers.
  • When required by law, court order or government request, or to protect the rights and safety of Reign, our users or others.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to affected users.
  • With your consent.

None of these include sharing SMS opt-in data or consent, as stated in section 4.

7. Data retention

We keep account data while the account is active. When a business closes its account, its data is kept for 30 days so the closure can be undone or an export requested, then deleted or anonymized, except records we must keep by law (for example, billing records for tax purposes). You may ask for earlier deletion at support@getreignos.com.

8. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access controls, multi-factor sign-in for business accounts and regular security reviews. No system is completely secure; if you suspect a problem with your account, contact us right away.

9. Cookies

We use cookies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to protect your session. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of the Service may stop working.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain uses. Clients of a business on Reign can also contact that business directly. To make a request, email support@getreignos.com; we respond within 30 days.

11. Children

The Service is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 and will delete it if we learn we have.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and notify business owners of material changes by email or in the app.
