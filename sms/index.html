Booking Text Messages

How clients opt in, what they receive, and how to stop

How clients opt in

Businesses on Reign OS, operated by ReignOS LLC, take bookings on their own Reign booking page. When booking, a client can choose to receive appointment texts by entering a mobile number and checking the consent box. The box is unchecked by default, and booking works the same without it. Messages clients receive

Only transactional messages about the client's own bookings. No marketing.

Confirmation: Reign OS: Your appointment at Luxe Studio is confirmed for Tue, Nov 18 at 2:00 PM. Details: https://app.getreignos.com/book/luxe-studio. Reply STOP to opt out.
Reminder: Reign OS: Reminder: Luxe Studio tomorrow at 2:00 PM. Need to change it? https://app.getreignos.com/book/luxe-studio. Reply STOP to opt out.
Cancellation: Reign OS: Your Luxe Studio appointment on Nov 18 was canceled. Rebook: https://app.getreignos.com/book/luxe-studio. Reply STOP to opt out.

Program details

  • Program name: Reign OS booking notifications, by ReignOS LLC.
  • Message frequency: Varies, usually 2–4 messages per booking.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP to any message. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Help: Reply HELP, or email support@getreignos.com.
  • Carriers: Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Privacy: Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.

Automatic replies

After opting in: Reign OS: You're subscribed to booking texts from Luxe Studio. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
HELP: Reign OS booking texts. Help: support@getreignos.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.
STOP: Reign OS: You're unsubscribed and won't get more texts. Reply START to resubscribe.
